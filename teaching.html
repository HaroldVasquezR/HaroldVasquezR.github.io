Computational Macroeconomics Laboratory

A shared computational laboratory for Macroeconomics I and II, focused on reproducible economic modeling and numerical analysis.

Students begin with common computational foundations and growth models before progressing to business cycles and macroeconomic policy applications.

The laboratory uses GNU Octave, with Dynare and complementary tools introduced progressively.
