Selected Projects

Research and technology initiatives combining economic modeling, financial data infrastructure, and artificial intelligence. The projects below are at different stages of development.

01

Macroeconomic research

GDP Nowcasting

Development of an open-source framework for estimating economic activity in the Dominican Republic using timely macroeconomic indicators and reproducible econometric methods. The project aims to integrate data acquisition, preprocessing, model estimation, and forecast evaluation into a transparent computational workflow, supporting regular updates of GDP nowcasts and comparisons across alternative modeling approaches.

Methods & Technologies: R · Time-series econometrics · Macroeconomic indicators · Forecast evaluation · Reproducible research

Development Status: Research and development

02

Data infrastructure

Financial & Macroeconomic Data Infrastructure

Development and testing of a modular data infrastructure for retrieving, organizing, and analyzing financial-market and macroeconomic information from multiple sources. Connectivity to financial-market data has been developed and is undergoing testing. Current work includes evaluating market-monitoring indicators and extending reproducible data workflows with standardized instrument tickers, economic-series identifiers, and source-specific connectors.

Methods & Technologies: Financial-market data connectivity · Data pipelines · Market-monitoring indicators (under evaluation) · Economic-series identifiers · OpenBB (planned integration)

Development Status: Testing · Financial-market connectivity developed · Monitoring indicators under evaluation

03

Artificial intelligence

Economic & Financial Intelligence Agent

Development of an AI-assisted research environment designed to support economic analysis, financial-market monitoring, and structured information processing. The project combines a self-hosted agent infrastructure with plans for specialized workflows involving data retrieval, research synthesis, and analytical reporting. The general-purpose agent environment has been configured, while domain-specific capabilities for economic and financial intelligence remain under development.

Methods & Technologies: AI agents · Self-hosted VPS · Python (planned integrations) · Research automation · Financial information processing

Development Status: Core infrastructure configured · Specialized capabilities in development

04

Cross-sector innovation

Med-AI

AI-Powered Medical Assistant & Health Records Management

A collaborative project developing an AI-assisted platform for clinical documentation, patient record management, and healthcare information retrieval. The proposed system aims to reduce manual data entry, organize longitudinal patient histories, and assist healthcare professionals in reviewing clinical information. The project also considers conversational AI capabilities that would allow physicians to interact with patient records and supporting documentation.

Methods & Technologies: Artificial intelligence · Clinical documentation · Health records · Document processing · Conversational interfaces

Development Status: Collaborative project · Development stage

Projects are presented at different stages of research, testing, and development. Additional documentation and resources will be shared as they become available.
