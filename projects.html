Selected Projects

Research and technology initiatives combining economic modeling, financial data infrastructure, and artificial intelligence. The projects below are at different stages of development.

01

Macroeconomic research

GDP Nowcasting

Development of an open-source framework for estimating economic activity in the Dominican Republic using timely macroeconomic indicators and reproducible econometric methods. The project integrates data processing, model estimation, and forecast evaluation to support regularly updated GDP nowcasts and comparisons across alternative modeling approaches.

Methods & Technologies: R · Time-series econometrics · Macroeconomic indicators · Forecast evaluation

Development Status: Research and development

02

Data infrastructure

Financial & Macroeconomic Data Infrastructure

Development and testing of a modular infrastructure for retrieving, organizing, and analyzing financial-market and macroeconomic data. Financial-market connectivity has been developed and is undergoing testing, while market-monitoring indicators and additional data integrations are being evaluated.

Methods & Technologies: Financial data connectivity · Data pipelines · Economic-series identifiers · OpenBB (planned)

Development Status: Testing and development

03

Artificial intelligence

Economic & Financial Intelligence Agent

Development of an AI-assisted environment for economic research, financial-market monitoring, and analytical reporting. The self-hosted agent infrastructure is operational, while specialized capabilities for data retrieval, research synthesis, and economic and financial intelligence remain under development.

Methods & Technologies: AI agents · Self-hosted VPS · Research automation · Financial information processing

Development Status: Core infrastructure operational · Specialization in development

04

Cross-sector innovation

Med-AI

AI-Powered Medical Assistant & Health Records Management

A collaborative project developing an AI-assisted platform for clinical documentation and patient record management. The system aims to automate data entry, organize longitudinal patient histories, and enable physicians to interact with clinical records through conversational AI.

Methods & Technologies: Artificial intelligence · Clinical documentation · Health records · Conversational interfaces

Development Status: Development stage

Projects are presented at different stages of research, testing, and development. Additional documentation and resources will be shared as they become available.
