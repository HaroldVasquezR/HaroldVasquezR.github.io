Macroeconomics I

Economic Growth

Course Overview

This course studies the determinants of long-run economic growth, including capital accumulation, population growth, technological progress, and intertemporal decisions.

Main Topics

Growth Foundations

Cobb–Douglas production, growth accounting, and stylized facts.

Solow Model

Capital accumulation, population growth, technological progress, and steady states.

AK Model

An introduction to endogenous growth.

Ramsey Model

Optimal saving, consumption, and intertemporal dynamics.

Romer Model

Ideas, innovation, and sustained economic growth.

Computational Laboratory

The computational component builds on the common Octave foundation and will progressively incorporate growth-model simulations.

Teaching materials and laboratory instructions are primarily in Spanish.
