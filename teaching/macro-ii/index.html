Macroeconomics II

Business Cycles and Policy

Course Overview

This course studies economic fluctuations, macroeconomic shocks, monetary policy, inflation dynamics, and stabilization using analytical and computational models.

Main Topics

Business-Cycle Facts

Output, employment, inflation, interest rates, and economic shocks.

IS–MP Framework

Aggregate demand, monetary policy, and transmission mechanisms.

Phillips Curve

Inflation dynamics and the interaction between prices and economic activity.

RBC Models

An introduction to productivity shocks and intertemporal decisions.

DSGE and Policy Models

Basic DSGE concepts and an introduction to quarterly projection models.

Computational Laboratory

The computational component builds on the common Octave foundation. Dedicated business-cycle and policy modules will be added progressively.

Teaching materials and laboratory instructions are primarily in Spanish.
